Miami Sunrise Realty

Demo page for a real estate company. In the bottom-right corner is the AI agent, embedded with a single <script> line. Click 💬 and try, for example: “Looking for a 2-bed condo under $650k in Brickell”.

How to embed on your own site: 
<script src="/widget.js" data-tenant="demo" data-api=""></script>

Each company gets its own data-tenant — its own city, currency, language and listings. A second example tenant berlin (EUR, German) ships in the repo.
